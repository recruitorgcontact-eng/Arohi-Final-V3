Account & User Data Deletion Request

App: Arohi AI | Developer: Braga Technologies Private Limited

App & Developer Verification

This page details the official account and data deletion process for users of the Arohi AI mobile application (Package ID: com.arohiai.app) and the web platform at arohiai.com, published and operated by Braga Technologies Private Limited in association with ODITREE SERVICES.

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of 2023, users can permanently delete their accounts and all associated private records at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Users have two quick options to delete their account and associated data:

Option 1: In-App Self-Service (Instant)

1. Open the Arohi AI app or visit arohiai.com/privacy.
2. Scroll to Section 5: Account & Personal Data Deletion Policy.
3. If signed in, tap the red "Delete My Account" button and type DELETE to confirm.
4. Your authentication credentials, documents, and profile data will be permanently purged immediately.

Option 2: Online Deletion Request Form (No Login Required)

If you do not have the app installed or cannot sign in, fill out the form below with your registered email address. Our data compliance officer will locate and permanently purge all associated records within 72 hours.

✓ Your deletion request has been recorded. All personal identifiers, test records, and authentication credentials will be purged from our databases within 72 hours.
Option 3: Direct Email Request

You may also email our Data Privacy Officer directly from your registered email address:
• support@arohiai.com
• elitetraderjunoon@gmail.com
Subject Line: "Arohi AI - Account & Data Deletion Request". Please mention your registered email or phone number.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

1. Data That is Permanently Deleted:

  • Authentication Credentials: User ID, hashed credentials, Google OAuth profile links, email address, and phone number.
  • Profile & Career Data: User full name, bio, target examinations, academic qualifications, and target state/location preferences.
  • User-Generated Content: Saved ATS Word/PDF resumes, mock interview recordings, audio logs, test performance scores, and notes.
  • AI Interaction History: Chat transcripts and queries exchanged with the Arohi AI conversational model.
  • Device Identifiers: Push notification tokens and session cookies.

2. Data That May Be Retained & Retention Period:

  • Financial & Transaction Records: In accordance with statutory Indian taxation (GST) and accounting regulations, records of paid subscriptions or invoice receipts are retained for the legally required period of 7 years. These records contain zero biometric, authentication, or career profile information.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention Logs: Anonymized server request timestamps with zero personally identifiable information (PII) may be stored for up to 90 days for infrastructure security audits.

Turnaround Time: All user account data deletion requests are completely executed within 72 hours of receipt.
